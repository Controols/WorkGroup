Our story

A different kind of textile partner.

Linen Works started after years of working alongside restaurants, cafés and hotels. Over time, we got to know the industry well. We saw how important textiles are to daily operations, but also how little attention is often given to the people using them every day.

We started Linen Works because we felt there was room for a different kind of textile partner — one that works closely with its clients and understands that every hospitality business operates differently.

Our founder's background as a cabinetmaker has shaped much of that thinking. Working with materials teaches you that there is rarely one solution that fits every situation. Different materials behave differently, and the best result depends on understanding how they are used, maintained and cared for over time. The same applies to textiles.

Today, Linen Works provides linen rental, commercial laundry and textile management for hospitality businesses across Zealand. We're proud to be an independent company, built from the ground up and shaped by the businesses we work with every day — because the best partnerships are built through consistency, transparency and a shared commitment to getting things right.

Why Linen Works

Service built on relationships.

Independent

We're an independent company built from the ground up. That lets us stay close to our clients, respond quickly and make decisions without layers of administration.

Flexible

Business doesn't always go to plan. Occupancy changes, events get added, demand shifts with little notice. We adapt and help our clients find solutions when circumstances change.

Transparent

Clear communication, straightforward pricing and honest conversations are part of how we work. We believe long-term partnerships are built on trust.

Accessible

When challenges arise, we want clients to feel comfortable picking up the phone. Good service starts with being available and finding solutions together — not hiding behind processes.

Doing things properly

Responsibility starts with everyday decisions.

We believe responsibility isn't something reserved for annual reports. It's reflected in everyday decisions — how people are treated, how resources are used, how commitments are honoured, and how a business chooses to operate when nobody is watching.

Creating opportunities

We believe people should be able to contribute, grow and build a future through meaningful work. Through partnerships with local employment initiatives, we support pathways into employment for people entering or re-entering the workforce. Not a programme or a target — simply something we believe in doing when we can.

A second life for textiles

A textile isn't finished because it can no longer serve its original purpose. Where possible we extend the life of textiles through repair, repurposing and responsible reuse — smaller items like napkins and kitchen towels become practical everyday products. If something can still serve a purpose, it shouldn't be discarded.

Local relationships

As an independent company operating across Zealand, we believe strong local relationships create better service, better communication and greater accountability. Our clients know who they're working with — and good partnerships are built through trust, accessibility and consistency over time.
