Our story

Built around one standard.

Cleaning Works is part of Works Group, alongside Linen Works. Keeping properties to standard has always gone hand in hand with the textiles we look after — so we built cleaning into a dedicated company, with its own team and its own focus.

We clean for hospitality and commercial businesses across Zealand, from boutique city-centre hotels to restaurants, offices and care facilities. Every service is managed in-house. No subcontracting, no agency cleaners passing through — the same trained people, to the same standard, every visit.

That matters in practice: when something needs fixing, there is one call to make.
